# Sources — Princeton ROB 345

Every lecture video, slide deck, set of notes and assignment that the public course website
(<https://irom-lab.princeton.edu/intro-to-robotics/>) links to, with its canonical URL. Enrolled
Princeton students use Canvas instead; this page covers only the public site.

The course hosts its documents on Dropbox, not on the course site itself. The PDFs are **not**
committed to this repo. The KB is read by an agent that navigates markdown, so the URL is the
useful part, and the Lecture 1 deck alone is 75MB. Cite these URLs directly. A KB column entry
means that lecture's material has been transcribed into this repo.

Inventory taken 2026-10-20 and checked again on 2026-10-28. The site lists 24 lectures. On
2026-10-28 materials were posted for lectures 1–11 (lectures 10 and 11 with a video and notes and no
slides); lectures 12–24 had only a title.

## Hardware (named on the course site)

The Crazyflie 2.1 (brushless) drone from Bitcraze (<https://www.bitcraze.io/>), the Flow deck v2
and the Crazyradio PA, plus a digital FPV camera and long pins for the final project.
